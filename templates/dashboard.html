Manual control · test by hand

manual: off

1) choose MANUAL (password), 2) press START motors (password), 3) hold a button or W A S D / arrow keys to drive. To go back to automatic line following: press AUTO (this stops the motors), then START. Release and it stops within half a second. Speed follows the Max speed slider and ramps smoothly. Camera obstacle detection is not enforced in manual mode: you are the safety.

Triggered keeps the last ~8 s before a problem (line lost, STUCK / OFF_LINE, obstacle or your mark) plus ~3 s after it, plus a slow baseline of ordinary frames, so you see what led up to the fault without filling the disk. Continuous saves every Nth frame at the chosen rate, as before. Saves the raw camera frames and a log row per frame (line offset / angle / lookahead / curvature, obstacle state, the decision, speeds, wheel PWM and encoder speeds, and where the line was drawn). Frames where the line is lost, tracking is STUCK / OFF_LINE or an obstacle is flagged are always saved, together with the detector's own views, so you can see what it got wrong. Press Mark wrong detection (or the M key) on any frame you disagree with. It works with the motors off, so you can also roll the rover by hand over the tape. Limits: 3 GB, 60 min or 3 GB free disk per session; the files are in recordings/.
